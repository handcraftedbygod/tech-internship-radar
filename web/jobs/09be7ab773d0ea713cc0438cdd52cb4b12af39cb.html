Junior Data Analyst Apprentice

QA — Elland, West Yorkshire

Apply on freehire

Terberg DTS is the UK subsidiary of the Royal Terberg Group, specialising in the supply, rental and support of specialist vehicles and equipment for logistics, ports, airports, industrial, rail, and fire & rescue sectors. The company combines vehicle sales with nationwide maintenance, parts and 24/7 support services to help customers maximise fleet up time.

Purpose of the role

The Junior Data Analyst is responsible for providing valuable business insight through the analysis of complex datasets and the creation and maintenance of reports. The role works closely with departments across the business to answer queries from managers and supervisors, supporting informed decision-making and continuous business improvement.

Key responsibilities

Create reporting packages and data models using Microsoft Excel, Power Query, Power Pivot and Power BI to generate meaningful insights from raw data.
