Graduate Data Engineer Trainee – 2-Year Apprenticeship

Wigan Council — Wigan, United Kingdom

Apply on freehire

Wigan Council is seeking a Graduate Trainee within the Data Integration Team to transform how data is used to improve services for residents. You will work with real data, systems and services; supporting data integration, analysis, reporting and digital innovation projects.

You will undertake a Level 5 Data Engineer Apprenticeship and Microsoft Fabric certification while developing data engineering, programming and analytics skills for evidence-based decision making across the organisation.
