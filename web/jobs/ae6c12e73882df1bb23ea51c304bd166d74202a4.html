Cybersecurity Intern (m/w/d)

Trusteq Gmbh — München

Apply on arbeitnow

YOUR TASKSYou will assist in the preparation and execution of projects related to risk management, identity and access management, the EUDI wallet, digital identities, DevSecOps, and AI security for our clients 

You will contribute to the development and implementation of security strategies and solutions 

You will work on internal research and development projects in the field of cybersecurity 

You will support the team in the practical development of innovative ideas and topics in the field of cybersecurity  

You will conduct research to prepare client presentations and proposals

You will document security measures, prepare reports, and present findings

YOUR PROFILYou are currently studying or already have a background in one of the following fields: (Business) Computer Science, Economics, Software Development, Data Science, Data Engineering, or a comparable discipline

You have some experience or knowledge in the areas of digital identities, Identity & Access Management (IAM), and the EUDI Wallet

Preferably, you have gained initial experience with common security tools as well as relevant cybersecurity regulations (e.g., NIS2 and DORA)

You have a solid foundational understanding of IT security and are passionate about digital identities

You possess analytical thinking skills and problem-solving abilities 

You work independently and in a structured manner 

You are a team player, motivated, hardworking, flexible, communicative, and proactive 

You enjoy interdisciplinary collaboration and taking on responsibility 

You have very good German and English language skills (at least C1 in both languages)

YOUR BENEFITSSupportive Team Culture: Team cohesion is important to us - we support one another and foster a healthy office culture. Our event team organizes regular after-work events to encourage exchange and connection beyond the workplace.

Early Ownership & Responsibility: From day one, you will take on exciting tasks with real responsibility – whether on your project or by contributing to internal initiatives. You help shape the way we work.

Clear Career Development Paths: You will find an environment that supports, challenges, and advances your professional growth. We offer clearly defined career paths and a transparent competency framework for every level.

Creative Work Environment: We give you the freedom and time to develop creative solutions and provide the best, most modern hardware to support you (Apple or Windows).

Employee Benefits: Flexible working models, a BahnCard, JobRad, Spendit card, Wellpass, and a company pension scheme contribute to your well-being. In addition, you’ll enjoy the best coffee from our new espresso machine and a fully stocked soft drinks fridge in the office.

Attractive Vacation Policy: You benefit from 30 days of paid vacation per year and the flexibility to work from home.
